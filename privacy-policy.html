Lumiflip privacy policy

Lumiflip – Flip Clock & Focus for Windows. Effective 31 October 2026.

In short

Lumiflip has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you set up stays on your PC. The app only goes online for the weather and for a calendar link, and only if you turn those on.

What stays on your PC

Your settings, alarms, events, focus history, imported calendar entries and any wallpaper pictures you add are stored in Lumiflip’s folder on your PC. They are never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling Lumiflip removes them.

What goes online, and only when you use it

  • Weather (optional). When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service. When weather is on, the chosen city’s location (latitude and longitude) is sent to Open-Meteo about every 15 minutes to get the forecast. Open-Meteo does not require an account. See Open-Meteo’s terms.
  • Calendar (optional). If you paste a calendar link, Lumiflip downloads that calendar directly from the service that hosts it (for example Microsoft Outlook or Google Calendar), about every 30 minutes. The link and your meetings stay on your PC. An imported .ics file never leaves your PC.
  • Talking clock (optional). The time is spoken by the voices built into Windows. If you pick a voice marked “Online”, Windows sends the phrase to Microsoft’s speech service to read it aloud.

The photos, fonts and scenes are part of the app, so showing them needs no internet connection.

Microsoft Store

If you installed Lumiflip from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles downloads, updates and any crash reports under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer only sees totals such as install counts and anonymous crash reports.

Children

Lumiflip collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
